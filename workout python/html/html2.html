    • this is Dashashi Canyon

    Dashbashi Canyon (also known as Tsalka Canyon) is one of the most stunning and popular natural landmarks in Georgia. Located in the Kvemo Kartli region near the village of Dashbashi, it sits roughly 90-100 kilometers (a 1.5 to 2-hour drive) from Tbilisi.

    Why should you visit this canyon?

    1. The Breathtaking "Weeping Wall" Waterfalls: The canyon floor hides a spectacular natural wonder—a lush, moss-covered cliff where water cascades down in hundreds of small streams. This creates a vibrant emerald oasis in the summer and a magical kingdom of frozen ice structures during the winter.
    2. The Thrilling Diamond Glass Bridge: Walking across the 240-meter-long transparent bridge suspended high above the gorge is an unforgettable experience. Having a coffee or drink inside the giant glass diamond hanging right in the middle offers some of the most unique, panoramic views in Georgia.
    3. Accessible Adventure & Day Trip Convenience: Located just a 1.5 to 2-hour drive from Tbilisi, it makes for the perfect day trip. It perfectly balances wild nature with modern excitement, allowing you to easily hike down a well-maintained trail to the river or try extreme activities like the Sky Bike and Giant Swing.
