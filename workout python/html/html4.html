    One of the famous Restourant

    Located directly inside the canyon complex. This is the most famous spot because part of it includes the multi-tiered glass diamond hanging right in the middle of the suspension bridge.

    Prices of food

    1. Main Dishes & Pizzas: Expect to pay around 35 to 50+ GEL
    2. Light Bites & Desserts: Appetizers, side dishes , soups, and desserts generally range from 15 to 30 GEL
    3. Important Bill Check: The menu prices listed do not include VAT. An extra 18% tax is added directly to your final check at the end of the meal.
