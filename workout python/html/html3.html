Simple Omelette Recipe

t is believed that omelettes originated in ancient Persia.According to Breakfast: A History, they were "nearly indistinguishable" from the Iranian dish kookoo sabzi, a Persian version of a frittata.

Prosporation time

  • Total: around 10 - 12 minute
  • proporation time:(3 - 5 minutes)
  • cooking: (2 - 5 minutes)

Ingredients

  • Eggs: 2 or 3 large eggs.
  • Butter: 1 teaspoon (or olive oil).
  • Cheese: 2-3 tablespoons (grated Cheddar, Mozzarella, or any melting cheese).
  • Salt: 1 pinch
  • Black Pepper: To taste.

Instructions

  1. Whisk the eggs: Crack 2 to 3 eggs into a bowl, add a pinch of salt and pepper, and beat vigorously with a fork until the yolks and whites are completely blended
  2. Heat the Pan: Place a non-stick skillet over medium heat, add 1 teaspoon of butter, and swirl it around until it is melted and coats the entire bottom of the pan
  3. Pour the mix: Pour the wundisturbed for just a few seconds until the bottom edge starts to set.
  4. Push the curds: Use a spatula to gently push the cooked edges toward the center while tilting the pan so the raw, liquid egg runs into the empty spaces.
  5. Add the cheese: When the top looks mostly set but still slightly wet and glossy, sprinkle your grated cheese evenly over one half of the omelette.
  6. Fold and slide: Fold the empty half over the cheese using your spatula, let it sit for 30 seconds so the cheese melts, and gently slide it onto your plate to serve.
